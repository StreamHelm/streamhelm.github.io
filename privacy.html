Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

This policy explains what StreamHelm ("the app") does with information from Google / YouTube, TikTok and Meta / Instagram accounts. The app is run by ThePepeSilviaShow on their own computer for their own channel.

Information the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read the channel's basic details - name, handle, avatar, channel ID (youtube.readonly).
  • TikTok: the account's basic profile (display name, username, avatar) and permission to upload / post videos (user.info.basic, user.info.profile, video.upload, video.publish).
  • Instagram / Facebook (Meta): the Instagram professional account linked to a Facebook Page (username, avatar, account ID), that Page's name, picture and Page token, and permission to publish Reels to the Instagram account and - if the owner turns it on - to that Facebook Page (instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, business_management, pages_manage_posts).

Each service has its own page with more detail: YouTube (Google) · TikTok · Instagram + Facebook (Meta) · Twitch · Discord · Spotify · OpenAI (GPT) · Anthropic (Claude) · Google Gemini.

How it is used

Only to upload the videos the owner picks, with the text the owner approves, to the owner's own accounts, and to show which account is connected. The app does not read, collect or analyse other people's data, private messages, comments, analytics or contacts.

Where it is stored

Sign-in tokens and account details are stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted with Windows' built-in data protection. They are never sent to a server run by the app's makers - there is none. Videos are uploaded directly from the owner's computer to the platform. If the owner sets up their own optional StreamHelm Cloud server (a server they rent or a Raspberry Pi they own), the chat bot's memory, its settings and the AI, Twitch and Discord keys the bot needs are copied to that server over an encrypted, signed connection; Google, TikTok and Meta sign-in tokens and videos are never copied there.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, rented or shared with anyone. When the owner asks the AI writer to suggest titles and captions, the clip's title, game name and - only if the owner switches it on - a few frames and the audio of that clip are sent to the AI provider the owner chose for that job (OpenAI, Anthropic or Google Gemini - each under its own API terms) to generate the text. During a Voice Chat with PepeBot, the streamer's microphone is recorded only while it is the streamer's turn to talk; each recording is sent to the chosen AI provider for transcription and is not stored. Lines viewers send for Speak as a Character are deleted from chat right away, kept only in memory until one is drawn, and discarded afterwards (the drawn line is sent to the chosen voice provider to be spoken). Characters' files (voice, bios, the lines chat gave them, talk summaries) stay on your computer in your Memory folder. Voice Lab recordings are sent to Google only when you create a voice and are not kept by StreamHelm. With SH.Voice on, Skyrim conversations (the game's context and your spoken lines) pass through StreamHelm to the AI service you picked and the exchanges are saved in the NPC's character file on your computer. In those conversations the microphone is recorded only when it is the player's turn and the game asks for it, and sent to the chosen AI provider for transcription. Always-on captions use Windows' built-in speech recognizer on the computer - that audio never leaves it. With the SH.Voice plugin, game conversations, player-character profiles and playthroughs stay in the Memory folder on your computer; xVASynth voices are made by xVASynth running on your own computer, so those lines never leave it; "Add a game" sends only the game's name and your note to the AI service you picked. No YouTube / Google account data, TikTok or Meta account data is ever sent to an AI provider.

Google API Services User Data Policy

StreamHelm's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays until the owner disconnects the account in the app (which deletes the stored tokens) or removes the app. See Data deletion for how to revoke access at any time.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is used only by its owner.
