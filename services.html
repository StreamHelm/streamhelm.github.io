Connected services

Every outside service StreamHelm can talk to, what it is used for, what it asks for, and how to connect it. Every service works with your own developer app today; for some, a shared StreamHelm app is planned so you can connect without any developer setup. We recommend the shared apps where available, but compare the options - your own app keeps you fully independent.

YouTube (Google)

Your own app: availableStreamHelm shared app: planned

Upload the clips you choose as YouTube Shorts / videos

TikTok

Your own app: availableStreamHelm shared app: planned

Send clips to the user's TikTok inbox as drafts, or post them directly

Instagram + Facebook (Meta)

Your own app: availableStreamHelm shared app: planned

Publish clips as Instagram Reels to the user's professional account

Twitch

Your own app: availableStreamHelm shared app: possible later

Read chat and events (EventSub) and post as the bot account

Discord

Your own app: availableStreamHelm shared app: possible later

Run PepeBot as a Discord bot (chat, moderation, alerts, events)

Spotify

Your own app: availableStreamHelm shared app: possible later

Now Playing overlay and !song

OpenAI (GPT)

Your own app: available

One of three AI frameworks the user can choose per job: the bot's replies and memory updates, clip titles / captions, clip watching and listening (optional), burned-in captions with word timings, trend lookups, the setup helper

Anthropic (Claude)

Your own app: available

One of three AI frameworks the user can choose per job: the bot's replies, events and memory, Discord chatbot, clip titles / captions, clip watching (images), trend lookups (web search), the setup helper

Google Gemini

Your own app: available

One of three AI frameworks the user can choose per job: replies, memory, clip titles / captions, clip watching and listening, trend lookups (Google Search grounding), the setup helper
