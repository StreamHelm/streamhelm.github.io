Data deletion

StreamHelm stores sign-in tokens only on its owner's computer. To remove them and revoke access:

  1. In the app: Socials page → Disconnect on the account (deletes the stored token immediately).
  2. Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions → StreamHelm → Remove access.
  3. TikTok: app → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions → StreamHelm → Remove.
  4. Facebook / Instagram: Facebook Settings → Business integrations → StreamHelm → Remove.

To ask for anything else to be deleted, email bleedblackngold1@gmail.com - requests are handled within 30 days.
