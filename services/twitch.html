Twitch

Your own app: availableStreamHelm shared app: possible later

What StreamHelm uses it for

  • Read chat and events (EventSub) and post as the bot account
  • Channel points, polls, predictions, shoutouts, clips, stream info, moderation

Permissions requested

  • chat, channel points, moderation, clips and channel scopes requested at login (listed on the Twitch consent screen)

Data

Sign-in tokens (encrypted) and per-viewer memory files (chat facts, counts) stored on the streamer's PC.

How to connect

  • Your own developer app - Your own developer app - you create a free app on the platform and paste its keys into StreamHelm's encrypted Key Vault. Works today; the app walks you through it step by step.
  • StreamHelm's shared app - PepeBot's shared app - connect in one click with the app the StreamHelm project runs. Planned: being set up and (where needed) reviewed by the platform.

Setup in short: Twitch developer app (Client ID) - device-code login, no redirect needed · Optional second login for the bot account

Remove access

  • https://www.twitch.tv/settings/connections
  • In StreamHelm: disconnect the account (deletes the stored token).
