Instagram + Facebook (Meta)

Your own app: availableStreamHelm shared app: planned

What StreamHelm uses it for

  • Publish clips as Instagram Reels to the user's professional account
  • Publish the same clips as Reels to the user's own Facebook Page (optional, per post or automatically)
  • Show which Instagram account / Facebook Page is connected

Permissions requested

  • instagram_basic
  • instagram_content_publish
  • pages_show_list
  • pages_read_engagement
  • business_management
  • pages_manage_posts

Data

A 60-day sign-in token and the Instagram username / avatar / account ID and linked Page name, stored encrypted on the user's PC only.

How to connect

  • Your own developer app - Your own developer app - you create a free app on the platform and paste its keys into StreamHelm's encrypted Key Vault. Works today; the app walks you through it step by step.
  • StreamHelm's shared app - PepeBot's shared app - connect in one click with the app the StreamHelm project runs. Planned: being set up and (where needed) reviewed by the platform.

Setup in short: Instagram Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page · Meta app (Business) with Facebook Login for Business + Instagram API · Development mode is enough for the app owner's own accounts · Public use by other people needs Meta App Review + Business Verification

Remove access

  • https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=business_tools
  • In StreamHelm: disconnect the account (deletes the stored token).
