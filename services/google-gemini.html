Google Gemini

Your own app: available

What StreamHelm uses it for

  • One of three AI frameworks the user can choose per job: replies, memory, clip titles / captions, clip watching and listening, trend lookups (Google Search grounding), the setup helper
  • Text-to-speech voices (30 voices)

Permissions requested

  • Gemini API key from Google AI Studio

Data

Only for the jobs the user assigns to Gemini: text prompts and - only when switched on - clip frames and audio. On Google's free tier Google may use prompts to improve its products; the paid tier does not. The key is stored encrypted on the user's PC.

How to connect

  • Your own developer app - Your own developer app - you create a free app on the platform and paste its keys into StreamHelm's encrypted Key Vault. Works today; the app walks you through it step by step.

Setup in short: Gemini API key (free tier or billing enabled in AI Studio) · Choose what it does on the AI Models page

Remove access

  • https://aistudio.google.com/apikey
  • In StreamHelm: disconnect the account (deletes the stored token).
