About StreamHelm

StreamHelm is a control center for streamers: one app that runs an AI co-host with a real memory, drives OBS, builds your scenes and overlays, handles alerts and channel points, runs a Discord bot, and turns your clips into Shorts, TikToks and Reels. It is built to save you time, make your stream feel like yours, and make your content stand out.

An AI co-host with a memory

PepeBot (rename him, give him your own picture) chats with your viewers in his own voice, remembers each of them across streams - facts, inside jokes, rivalries, music taste - and welcomes follows, subs, raids and cheers his way. Every feature can run Off, in Shadow (thinks but doesn't post) or Live.

The same bot on Twitch and Discord

One personality, one memory, two homes - with moderation, verification, stream alerts, events and a daily read on how your server is feeling.

Your whole stream from one screen

OBS runs in the background while the Stream Manager gives you a live preview, scenes, audio, go-live, stats, chat, mod tools and one-click quick actions in panels you arrange yourself.

Scenes and overlays without design software

A Canva-style Scene Builder with frames for your webcam and game, widgets, socials, text effects and animations, published to OBS in one click - plus alerts with TTS, Now Playing, a chat box and a Discord voice overlay.

Content that works for you after the stream

The Clip Studio turns Twitch clips (or any video) into vertical shorts with speed effects, voiceover, music, captions and text, writes titles and hashtags tuned for each platform, and posts to YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels.

Build your own features

The Feature Builder and quick actions let you make new commands and automations without code - or describe them and let the AI build them.

Why I built it

I love creating unique environments and spaces, and I love setting myself apart. A finely tuned, personal chatbot is a big part of that for me, and so are the unique features that make my stream feel like my stream.

I don't have a huge following, but I put a lot of time and energy into shaping the atmosphere for the people who are there. A goofy chatbot turned out to be something my regulars genuinely love - so I wanted to bring him into Discord too. Getting one bot to live in two places is what sent me down the path of building an app.

From there it kept growing. Help with content creation would be nice - so the Clip Studio and the socials tools came in. Then more ideas, and more: anything that makes my life easier, makes my stream better, or makes my content better. That is the whole driver behind this. I am a streamer and a content creator, and I built this with that in mind.

It started out personalized entirely to me. Once it was, I realized it could help a lot of other people on their own content-creation journey. It took me months to get a bot like this in place - and none of that needs to be that hard for the next person. StreamHelm streamlines it: AI built into the app to help you set things up, guided steps, a scene builder, all of it.

Everything here grew out of that one original idea - an AI chatbot with personality - and I couldn't be happier with where it has gone. I hope it saves you time and helps you stand out.

- ThePepeSilviaShow
