🎙 Voice Chat with PepeBot

A channel-point reward: PepeBot talks with the streamer out loud for a few minutes - he hears the mic and answers with his own voice.

  • Viewers can choose the topic; otherwise PepeBot picks one he has never used before
  • Listens only while it is the streamer's turn, with automatic "stopped talking" detection
  • Plays through the Alerts overlay so chat hears it
  • Afterwards he remembers the conversation: the streamer's and the viewer's memory, inside jokes, rivalries
  • Live transcript, timer, wrap-up and a list of past topics in the app
