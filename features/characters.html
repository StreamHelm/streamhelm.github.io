🧙 Characters + Talk hotkey

Every character chat voices gets its own voice, bios and memory - and you can talk with any of them (or PepeBot) from a hotkey, mid-game.

  • A folder per character, by game, plus General characters
  • Voices picked to fit them across every voice framework you use, then remembered
  • Real game characters get a bio from the web; made-up ones grow theirs from chat
  • Talk hotkey + popup menu over your game; press it again to end
  • Viewers can send a character to talk with you
