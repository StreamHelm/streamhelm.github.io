🗡 SH.Voice - game NPCs that talk back (plugin)

Talk to Skyrim's NPCs with your voice and hear them answer in game: one character per NPC, a persona and memories per playthrough, relationships, NPC actions, your own world lore, xVASynth voices, captions - and chat can whisper to them.

  • Its own Skyrim mod, made for your mod manager (Vortex / MO2) from the app - nothing else to run
  • G to talk, Y to make two NPCs talk to each other without you
  • Who likes (or avoids) talking to whom, and pairs you queue from the app
  • Your character is their own persona, per save
  • Relationships that form and change as you play
  • xVASynth game voices + lip sync
  • Your world lore in switchable pieces, and a faith for every NPC
  • NPCs act on what they say: follow you, attack, forgive, trade, leave
  • Add more games with an AI-drafted setup menu
