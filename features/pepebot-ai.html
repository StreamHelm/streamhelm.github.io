🐸 PepeBot - the AI co-host

A chat bot with a personality and a long memory that talks with viewers, remembers them across streams and helps run the show.

  • Answers when someone says "pepe bot" or replies to him; follows conversations
  • Per-viewer memory: facts, inside jokes, rivalries, music taste, multi-day "earlier streams" memory
  • Welcomes follows, subs, gift subs, raids and cheers in his own words
  • Shoutouts, polls, predictions and ad-break announcements
  • Every feature can be Off, Shadow (thinks but doesn't post) or Live
  • Response length, model and temperature per feature; a monthly AI budget
