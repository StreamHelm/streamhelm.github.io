🎬 Clip Studio

Turn Twitch clips (or any video) into vertical shorts and post them to YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels.

  • Layouts: horizontal, blurred fit, 1-4 stacked areas, 2×2 grid
  • Quick edit: speed / timelapse / slow-mo, looks, fades, zoom, mirror
  • Voiceover (record or import), background music, text and captions from speech
  • AI writes titles, descriptions and hashtags - optionally watching and listening to the clip, checking current trends, and following your directions
  • Post directly, as a draft, or export & open the upload page
