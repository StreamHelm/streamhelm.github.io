📈 Chat Pulse

An overlay of what chat keeps repeating - words, phrases and emotes with running tallies - plus chat votes and an AI choose-your-own-adventure.

  • Word cloud, leaderboard, bubbles or ticker, with presets and full styling
  • Running tallies (x12) that fade out when chat moves on; spam-proof (each person counts once)
  • Chat decides: votes chat answers by typing an option or its number
  • AI adventure: PepeBot narrates, chat votes what happens next each round
