Pepe's Helm Friends — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-31

Pepe's Helm Friends ("the app") is run by Sky, the streamer of ThePepeSilviaShow ("we"). It is available as a Twitch panel extension on ThePepeSilviaShow's channel and as a web app at https://streamhelm.github.io/helm-friends/. Contact: bleedblackngold1@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your Twitch user ID, login name and display name, and the date your Twitch account was created (used to stop brand-new accounts from farming the currency).
  • Your role on the channel (viewer, moderator or broadcaster).
  • What you create and do in the app: your character's look, the items you unlocked, your Pepe's Silvia Dollahs balance and transaction history, the channel games and predictions you joined, and the rewards you redeemed.
  • When you were last seen in chat or in the viewer list (times only, never your messages).
  • For web app logins only: a random session code (we store only a scrambled version of it) and your browser type, so you can stay logged in for up to 30 days.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your email, your messages, your payment details or your Twitch password, and we do not keep your Twitch access token. When you log in on the web app, Twitch's token is used once to confirm who you are and is then thrown away. We do not store IP addresses; they are held in memory for at most 10 minutes to prevent abuse.

Why

Only to run the app on ThePepeSilviaShow: to show your character on stream, keep your balance and unlocks, and prevent cheating. We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where

On a private server rented by ThePepeSilviaShow (UpCloud us-nyc1), plus backups on the streamer's own computer. Data travels over encrypted HTTPS.

How long

As long as you use the app. Accounts that are inactive for 24 months are deleted. Server backups roll off after 14 days.

Your choices

You can hide your character from the stream at any time, and you can delete your data in Settings → "Delete my data" (or by emailing us). Deleting removes your name and your character. Transaction records are kept only in an anonymous form, so the channel's balances still add up.

Children

Twitch requires users to be at least 13. The app is meant for Twitch users only.

Twitch

Twitch's own privacy notice applies to your use of Twitch: twitch.tv/p/legal/privacy-notice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and mention it on the app's Help page.
